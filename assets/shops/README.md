# Shop photographs

These are representative photographs of the 69 catalogue shops. The source
page, original public image URL, source credit, caption and image type are
recorded per shop in `data/shop-photos.json`.

Sources include store and operator websites, official facility and tourism
pages, original shop interviews and public visitor photo pages. Google Maps
temporary photo URLs and unrelated stock photographs are not used. Where a
current space photograph could not be confirmed, a photograph of that shop's
food or drink is used and labelled as such. The two `collage` entries are official
photographic compositions, not generated store images.

Photographs were visually checked on 2026-10-11. They are resized to at most
960 px without cropping and encoded as WebP. `scripts/photo-assets.py download`
can rebuild the assets from their recorded original URLs (Python 3 and FFmpeg
are required). Older editorial photographs can differ from the current shop.
